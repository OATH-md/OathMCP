---
title: Privacy and data
description: Minimize clinical data and preserve the deployment boundary around OathMCP.
---

OathMCP is stateless and calculators require structured values, not patient
identity. Do not send names, record numbers, free-text notes, or other protected
or personal health information unless a deployment has been specifically
designed and approved for that data.

The deployer remains responsible for authentication, authorization, transport
security, network exposure, origin policy, monitoring, abuse prevention,
availability, consent, retention, deletion, audit logging, incident response,
contractual safeguards, and applicable regulatory obligations.

Open-source availability does not make a deployment compliant, secure, approved,
or suitable for clinical use.
