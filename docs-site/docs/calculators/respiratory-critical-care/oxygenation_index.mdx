## Inputs

| Field | Type | Required | Default | Plausible | Hard limits | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `mean_airway_pressure` | number | Yes | — | 3–50 | 0.1–100 | Mean airway pressure in cmH2O. |
| `fio2` | number | Yes | — | — | 21–100 | Fraction of inspired oxygen as a percentage (21 to 100). |
| `pao2` | number | No | — | 20–600 | 1–700 | Arterial oxygen partial pressure (PaO2) in mmHg. Provide this or SpO2. |
| `spo2` | number | No | — | 85–95 | 40–100 | Pulse-oximetry oxygen saturation (SpO2) as a percentage. Provide this or PaO2. |

## Outputs

| Field | Type | Availability | Primary | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `oi` | number | When any of: pao2 | Yes | OI = (MAP × FiO2 × 100)/PaO2, rounded to 2 decimal places (present when PaO2 given). |
| `osi` | number | When any of: spo2 | Yes | OSI = (MAP × FiO2 × 100)/SpO2, rounded to 2 decimal places (present when SpO2 given). |
