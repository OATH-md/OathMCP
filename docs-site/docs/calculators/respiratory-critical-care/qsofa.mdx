### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** A poor-outcome prompt, not a sepsis diagnosis and not a sufficiently sensitive standalone sepsis screen. The 2026 Surviving Sepsis Campaign recommends NEWS, NEWS2, MEWS, or SIRS over qSOFA as a single inpatient screening tool. Do not use this adult model in children or as a replacement for SOFA and clinical assessment.
