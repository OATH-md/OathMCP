### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not use venous PCO2 for arterial ventilatory decisions or interpret any branch without the clinical context and laboratory reference interval.

- Peripheral venous PCO2 is not interchangeable with arterial PCO2, so compensation and respiratory mixed-disorder branches are suppressed for venous samples.
- Delta ratios are returned only when their anion-gap and bicarbonate prerequisites are met.
