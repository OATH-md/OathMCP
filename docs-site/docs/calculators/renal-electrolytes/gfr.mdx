### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not use in patients under 18, during acute kidney injury, as an exact GFR measurement, or when creatinine is changing rapidly or strongly affected by non-GFR factors. Confirm values near critical decisions with cystatin C or measured GFR.

- Do not use this adult creatinine-only estimate in patients under 18, in acute kidney injury or other non-steady-state creatinine conditions, or as a precise GFR measurement. It is less reliable when non-GFR determinants of creatinine are important.
