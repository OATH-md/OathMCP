### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not use as a fluid prescription or correction-rate target, in children, or without accounting separately for ongoing losses and gains.

- This static estimate excludes ongoing losses, gains, and urine electrolyte-free water clearance. It is not a fluid prescription or correction-rate target.
