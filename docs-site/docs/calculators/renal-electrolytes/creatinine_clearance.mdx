### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not silently choose actual, ideal, adjusted, or lean body weight. Do not use with non-steady-state creatinine, as measured clearance, or for KDIGO GFR-category staging.

- Do not use with changing serum creatinine, as a measured clearance, or for CKD GFR-category staging. Body-weight selection is not standardized; use the exact weight scalar required by the governing drug label or protocol.
