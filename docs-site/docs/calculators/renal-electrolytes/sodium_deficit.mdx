### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not use in children or as a sodium dose, saline volume, correction-rate target, or substitute for cause- and symptom-directed hyponatremia management.

- Hyponatremia is usually a disorder of water balance rather than a literal whole-body sodium deficit. This value is not a sodium dose, saline volume, correction-rate target, or treatment recommendation.
