## Clinical use

### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not infer a kidney-function method, cap, target AUC, or treatment protocol. Prefer measured GFR when estimated kidney function may be unreliable.

- Do not silently substitute Cockcroft-Gault CrCl for measured GFR or BSA-adjusted CKD-EPI eGFR. Measured GFR is preferred when estimates may be unreliable, including curative intent, extremes of body size or muscle mass, amputees, paraplegia, eGFR above 125, or eGFR at or below 45 mL/min/1.73 m².
- Verify target AUC, kidney-function method, cap policy, and rounding against the governing oncology protocol and pharmacy workflow.

## Warnings, constraints, and adjustments

- **treatment_intent when ==curative; where kidney_function_method ==ckd_epi_2009_no_race_egfr:** ADDIKD/eviQ prefers directly measured GFR for curative treatment intent.
- **Constraint (requiredWhen):** Patient BSA is required to convert indexed kidney function to absolute mL/min.
- **Constraint (requireValueWhen):** Directly measured GFR must be supplied as absolute mL/min; do not BSA-index it.
- **Constraint (requireValueWhen):** The NCI/CTEP estimated-kidney-function cap does not apply to directly measured GFR.
- **Constraint (requireValueWhen):** Cockcroft-Gault creatinine clearance must be supplied as absolute mL/min; do not BSA-index it.
