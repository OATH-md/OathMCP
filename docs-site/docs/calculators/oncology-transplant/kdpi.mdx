| Dimension | Declared contract |

## Warnings, constraints, and adjustments

- **donor_creatinine when &gt;8:** Serum creatinine exceeds 8 mg/dL and was capped at 8 mg/dL for the KDRI, per the OPTN KDPI methodology.
- **Adjustment `creatinine_cap_8`:** cap at 8 when its declared condition applies.
