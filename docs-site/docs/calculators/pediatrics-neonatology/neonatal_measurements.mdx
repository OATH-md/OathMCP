### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not treat insertion estimates as confirmed position or reference BP distributions as intervention thresholds.

- Estimates do not confirm final catheter or ETT position and blood-pressure ranges are descriptive references, not treatment targets.
- The original Shukla UVC formula has documented over-insertion risk and must be reported with that limitation.
