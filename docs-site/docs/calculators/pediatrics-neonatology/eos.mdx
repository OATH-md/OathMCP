### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not use below 35 weeks gestation or silently choose the 2024 model where universal antenatal GBS screening is not practiced. Clinical illness takes precedence over a reassuring numeric estimate.

- Do not use below 35 weeks gestation. Temperature is the highest maternal intrapartum temperature and baseline incidence must reflect the local population. A clinically ill infant requires immediate clinical evaluation; the numerical estimate is not a substitute for neonatal sepsis management.
