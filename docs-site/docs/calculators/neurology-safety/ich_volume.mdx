### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not calculate without all four CT measurements and the declared compatibility shape input.

- Accuracy decreases for large, irregular, lobar, multilobar, or heterogeneous hemorrhages and when the simplified slice count does not reproduce Kothari's weighted C method. Prefer planimetry or validated segmentation when precision affects a decision.
