### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not manufacture a total when any component is Not Testable, and do not generalize traumatic-brain-injury severity labels to other contexts.

- Intubation, tracheostomy, sedation, paralysis, communication barriers, and local ocular injury can make a component Not Testable. Never substitute 1 for an untestable component or report a misleading total.

## Warnings, constraints, and adjustments

- **Constraint (requireValueWhen):** Ocular obstruction requires the eye component to be recorded Not Testable.
- **Constraint (requireAtLeastValuesWhen):** The ocular-obstruction Not Testable option requires the matching or multiple-modifier context.
- **Constraint (requireValueWhen):** Intubation or tracheostomy requires the verbal component to be recorded Not Testable.
- **Constraint (requireAtLeastValuesWhen):** The intubation Not Testable option requires the matching or multiple-modifier context.
- **Constraint (requireValueWhen):** A communication barrier requires the verbal component to be recorded Not Testable.
- **Constraint (requireAtLeastValuesWhen):** The communication-barrier Not Testable option requires the matching or multiple-modifier context.
- **Constraint (requireValueWhen):** Paralysis requires the motor component to be recorded Not Testable.
- **Constraint (requireAtLeastValuesWhen):** The paralysis Not Testable option requires the matching or multiple-modifier context.
- **Constraint (requireAtLeastValuesWhen):** Multiple modifiers require at least two affected GCS components to be recorded Not Testable.
