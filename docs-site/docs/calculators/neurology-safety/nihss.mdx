### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Full of caveats for patients with prior neurologic deficits (prior weakness, hemi-/quadriplegia, blindness), intubation, or a language barrier. In those cases consult the NIH Stroke Scale reference. Not a substitute for clinical judgment or imaging.

## Warnings, constraints, and adjustments

- **Constraint (requireValueWhen):** Intubation requires dysarthria to be recorded UN rather than assigned a numeric score.
- **Constraint (requireAtLeastValuesWhen):** The intubated dysarthria option requires the matching or multiple-modifier context.
- **Constraint (requireAtLeastValuesWhen):** Amputation or joint fusion context requires at least one affected NIHSS item to be recorded UN.
- **Constraint (requireAtLeastValuesWhen):** An amputated left-arm item requires the matching or multiple-modifier context.
- **Constraint (requireAtLeastValuesWhen):** An amputated right-arm item requires the matching or multiple-modifier context.
- **Constraint (requireAtLeastValuesWhen):** An amputated left-leg item requires the matching or multiple-modifier context.
- **Constraint (requireAtLeastValuesWhen):** An amputated right-leg item requires the matching or multiple-modifier context.
- **Constraint (requireAtLeastValuesWhen):** An amputated ataxia item requires the matching or multiple-modifier context.
- **Constraint (requireAtLeastValuesWhen):** Multiple modifiers require at least two affected NIHSS items to be recorded UN.
