## Warnings, constraints, and adjustments

- **creatinine when &lt;1:** Creatinine below 1 mg/dL was floored to 1 mg/dL for MELD 3.0.
- **creatinine when &gt;3:** Creatinine above 3 mg/dL was capped at 3 mg/dL for MELD 3.0.
- **dialysis when ==true:** The dialysis criterion sets creatinine to 3 mg/dL for MELD 3.0.
- **bilirubin when &lt;1:** Bilirubin below 1 mg/dL was floored to 1 mg/dL for MELD 3.0.
- **inr when &lt;1:** INR below 1 was floored to 1 for MELD 3.0.
- **sodium when &lt;125:** Sodium below 125 mEq/L was floored to 125 mEq/L for MELD 3.0.
- **sodium when &gt;137:** Sodium above 137 mEq/L was capped at 137 mEq/L for MELD 3.0.
- **albumin when &lt;1.5:** Albumin below 1.5 g/dL was floored to 1.5 g/dL for MELD 3.0.
- **albumin when &gt;3.5:** Albumin above 3.5 g/dL was capped at 3.5 g/dL for MELD 3.0.
- **Constraint (requiredWhen):** Sex is required for the adult MELD 3.0 calculation.
- **Adjustment `creatinine_clamp_1_3`:** clamp at 1–3 when its declared condition applies.
- **Adjustment `dialysis_creatinine_3`:** clamp at 3 when its declared condition applies.
- **Adjustment `bilirubin_floor_1`:** floor at 1 when its declared condition applies.
- **Adjustment `inr_floor_1`:** floor at 1 when its declared condition applies.
- **Adjustment `sodium_clamp_125_137`:** clamp at 125–137 when its declared condition applies.
- **Adjustment `albumin_clamp_1_5_3_5`:** clamp at 1.5–3.5 when its declared condition applies.
- **Adjustment `meld_score_clamp_6_40`:** clamp at 6–40 when its declared condition applies.
