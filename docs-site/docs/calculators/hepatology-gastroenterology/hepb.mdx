### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not infer a complete pattern from omitted, unknown, or not-tested markers, and do not use HBe markers alone to label disease phase or infectivity.

- This tool does not establish disease phase, infectivity level, treatment, or prognosis.
- Unknown and not-tested markers remain distinct from negative results.
