## Clinical use

| Dimension | Declared contract |

| Population | Adults in a stable ambulatory setting who are at increased risk for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD), including adults with type 2 diabetes, overweight or obesity plus another metabolic risk factor, or incidental steatosis or elevated liver enzymes. |
| Setting | First-tier MASLD fibrosis-risk screening using contemporaneous age, AST, ALT, and platelet count. The selected 2026 AGA pathway treats an elevated age-adjusted FIB-4 as a positive screen requiring second-tier assessment, not as a diagnosis of fibrosis. |

### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not use during acute illness, with non-contemporaneous or unstable laboratory values, below age 18, or as a stand-alone diagnosis or fibrosis stage. This implementation intentionally does not apply the separate 2.67 referral threshold retained by AASLD 2023 and EASL 2024.

- Do not use FIB-4 in acutely ill patients or when aminotransferases or platelets do not represent a stable clinical state.
- FIB-4 has low accuracy below age 35; this calculator returns the raw score but does not interpret the MASLD screen in that age group.
- This MASLD screening variant is not a substitute for evaluation of other liver diseases, second-tier fibrosis testing, specialist assessment, or clinical judgment.
