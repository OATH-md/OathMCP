### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not use an admission subtotal as a completed score, silently select a variant, or infer modern individual mortality, necrosis, disposition, or treatment from this historical model.

- A completed score is unavailable before 48 hours or while an applicable observation is missing. Historical cohort mortality bands are not individualized predictions and do not determine necrosis, disposition, monitoring, or treatment.

## Warnings, constraints, and adjustments

- **Constraint (requireValueWhen):** The 48-hour assessment requires follow-up observations timestamped at hour 48.
- **Constraint (forbidPresentWhen):** Follow-up observations are not due before 48 completed hours.
- **Constraint (forbidPresentWhen):** Forty-eight-hour observations must be omitted before they are due.
- **Constraint (forbidPresentWhen):** Arterial pO2 is not a criterion in the gallstone variant and must be omitted.
