### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not convert a nonspecific CSF pattern or unidentified positive assay into a disease diagnosis.

- The tool does not produce a most-likely diagnosis, confidence score, treatment, or prognosis.
- Age, pretreatment, immune status, assay identity, paired serum testing, imaging, and xanthochromia method/timing materially limit interpretation.
