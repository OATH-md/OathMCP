### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not calculate below 60 inches (152.4 cm), without a formula sex input, or when the governing protocol specifies another size descriptor.

- The Devine formula is anchored at 60 inches (152.4 cm) and is not accepted below that height. It is not a substitute for adjusted or lean body weight when a dosing protocol specifies another size descriptor.
