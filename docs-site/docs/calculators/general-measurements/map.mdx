### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not calculate from a missing pressure or when systolic pressure is not greater than diastolic pressure.

- The 1/3–2/3 estimate assumes a normal heart rate and waveform; it is less accurate at extremes of heart rate or with significant arrhythmia, where a measured (invasive) MAP is preferred.
