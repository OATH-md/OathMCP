### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not calculate without establishing adult applicability and obtaining both measured height and measured weight.

- Do not apply these fixed adult categories to children. Interpret adult BMI with other health factors because it is a screening measure and surrogate marker rather than a direct body-fat measurement.
