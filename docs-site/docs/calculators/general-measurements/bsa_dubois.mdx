### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not calculate without a protocol naming the Du Bois variant and both measured height and measured weight.

- The 1916 formula was derived from a very small sample and may be inaccurate at body-size extremes. Apply any dosing cap only when the governing protocol explicitly requires it; this tool reports raw BSA.
