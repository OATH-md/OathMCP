### Exclusions and limitations

**When not to use:** Do not calculate without a protocol naming the Mosteller variant and both measured height and measured weight.

- BSA equations can diverge at body-size extremes. Use the exact formula and any cap named by the governing protocol; no single BSA formula is universally preferred.
